How to choose between them

Three questions settle most of the doubt, and none of them is about which platform is "the best":

  • What do you want to trade? Anyone focused on crypto needs a different catalog from someone trading forex.
  • How often? Anyone opening and closing on the same day feels the spread cost; anyone holding a position for weeks feels the swap.
  • Have you traded before? Someone just starting out gets more value from a demo account and simple sign-up than from a huge catalog.

Start with the demo account

Whatever you choose, the cheapest path is to open the account, test the platform with demo funds, and only then decide where to put real capital. Signing up costs nothing and does not require you to deposit.
