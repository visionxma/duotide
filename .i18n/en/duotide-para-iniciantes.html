Duotide for beginners

If you have never traded before, this is the most important page on the site — and it starts with a caveat that most content in this niche leaves out.

Duotide is not regulated by the CVM, Brazil's securities regulator. For beginners, this matters more than for an experienced trader: you cannot yet tell your own mistakes apart from platform problems, and you will not have a Brazilian regulator to turn to if something goes wrong. If institutional protection matters to you, start with a broker authorized in Brazil — or, if you live elsewhere, one authorized by your own country's financial regulator.

What to understand before anything else

  • Leverage multiplies both sides. It is sold as "trade more with less", and that is exactly what it is — including when you lose;
  • Most people lose. Brokers regulated in Europe are required to disclose this percentage, and it hovers around 70–80% of retail accounts losing money;
  • There is no such thing as a guaranteed return. Anyone who promises one is lying, without exception;
  • No one else should trade your account. An unauthorized manager trading other people's money is illegal, and it is the most common scam in the industry.

A sensible way in

  1. Weeks 1 to 4: use only the demo account, with a virtual balance equal to what you would actually invest;
  2. Write everything down: each trade, the reason for entering it and the result. Without a record there is no learning;
  3. After a month: open a live account with the minimum deposit;
  4. Test a withdrawal: take out part of it and time it. This step is not optional;
  5. Only then consider depositing more — and even then, only money you can afford to lose.

The mistakes that wipe out beginners' accounts

  • Doubling down after a loss to "win it back" — the fastest way to blow up your account;
  • Trading without a stop, hoping the price will come back;
  • Using high leverage at the start, when mistakes are still frequent;
  • Accepting bonuses without reading the volume requirement, then finding out you cannot withdraw;
  • Investing money you need — pressure for results makes every decision worse;
  • Following signals from social media groups, which are often affiliate bait or worse.

It is worth stating the obvious: this site earns a commission if you sign up. Even so, the honest advice for beginners is to go slowly, with little money, after testing a withdrawal. If that means you deposit less, so be it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Duotide good for beginners?

The platform has a simple sign-up and a demo account, which helps with learning. On the other hand, it is not regulated by the CVM and it offers leveraged products, which are high risk. Beginners looking for regulatory protection should consider an institution authorized in Brazil, or by the financial regulator in their own country.

How much money do I need to get started?

The minimum the platform accepts, and only an amount you can lose entirely without it affecting your life. Never use your emergency fund, rent money or borrowed funds.
