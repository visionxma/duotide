Duotide support: how to reach the team

At Duotide you’re never left without help: every client has an account manager available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. To get yours, just create an account. Below are all the ways to reach the team.

Before you start: check the FAQ

There's a good chance your question already has an answer. In the FAQ you'll find information about:

  • Deposits and withdrawals;
  • Account opening and verification;
  • Demo account and platform features.

Didn't find your answer? Use one of the channels below.

1. In-platform chat: the fastest way

Inside your account, open the support area and start a chat. You'll talk directly with the team, who already has access to your account's context and resolves most cases: technical questions, verification, deposits and withdrawals.

2. Email: for detailed cases

If your case needs more detail, send an email to suporte@duotide.com.br. It's the best option when:

  • The issue is more complex and needs analysis;
  • You want to explain the situation calmly and attach screenshots or documents;
  • You want a written record of the conversation.

To speed things up

Have on hand:

  • The email used at registration;
  • The date, time and amount of the trade or transaction;
  • Screenshots showing the situation.

Quick summary

  • FAQ: instant answers to the most common questions;
  • In-platform chat: the fastest channel, right inside your account;
  • Email: suporte@duotide.com.br, for detailed cases.

Whatever the channel, your account manager and the Duotide team are available 24/7. Create your account and get your manager right now.

Frequently asked questions

How do I contact Duotide support?

Through the chat in the platform's support area or by email at suporte@duotide.com.br. Support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What is the fastest channel?

The in-platform chat, because you're already logged in and the agent can see your account's context.

When is it better to use email?

For detailed cases that need attachments like documents and screenshots, or when you want a written record of the conversation.
