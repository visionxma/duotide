Frequently asked questions about Duotide

We have gathered the most recurring questions about Duotide and about unregulated brokers in Brazil — including the ones almost no site in the industry answers honestly, like income tax and what to do when a withdrawal will not go through.

Is Duotide regulated by the CVM?

No. Duotide is not listed as authorized by the Comissão de Valores Mobiliários to offer brokerage services to residents of Brazil, and it declares registration in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG).

Is it illegal to use a foreign broker?

For an individual, investing abroad is not illegal. What the law restricts is actively offering these services in Brazil without authorization. You must, however, declare the assets and gains to Brazil's tax authority (Receita Federal).

Do I need to declare it on my income tax return?

Yes. Gains from trading abroad and assets held outside the country have their own reporting and payment rules. Consult an accountant — the responsibility falls on the investor, regardless of whether the broker reports it or not.

What does it mean for a broker to be registered in SVG?

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is a jurisdiction with low regulatory requirements for financial companies. Registration proves the company legally exists, but it is not the same as market supervision like the CVM carries out in Brazil or CySEC in Europe.

What is the difference between a regulated and an unregulated broker?

A regulated broker is subject to capital requirements, audits, segregation of client money and a formal complaints channel. With an unregulated one, none of that is verifiable by you, and there is no authority to turn to if something goes wrong.

What is leverage, and why is it risky?

Leverage lets you trade an amount larger than your balance. It multiplies gains and losses in the same proportion: with 100x leverage, a 1% move against your position wipes out the capital applied to it.

How much money do I need to start trading?

Just the platform's minimum deposit, and only an amount you can afford to lose entirely. The first deposit is there to test whether the broker pays out withdrawals, not to generate profit.

Why do brokers offer deposit bonuses?

Because the bonus is usually tied to a trading volume requirement before it allows a withdrawal. That keeps the money on the platform and increases turnover. Read the terms before accepting one — it can lock your withdrawal for weeks.

How do I know if a broker's website is fake?

Check the address in your browser character by character, be suspicious of links received by message, and always access the site by typing the address or using a saved bookmark. Cloning the login screen is the most common scam in the industry.

Can I trust buy and sell signals from groups?

Treat them with a lot of caution. Most of these groups are paid through affiliate commissions and earn from the volume you trade, regardless of whether you profit or lose. A paid signal that promises a high hit rate is, in practice, always a scam.

What should I do if a broker will not pay my withdrawal?

Open a formal ticket and keep the reference number, gather every piece of proof, file a complaint at consumidor.gov.br and with Reclame Aqui (Brazilian consumer complaint platforms), and seek legal advice. Without CVM authorization, there is no Brazilian regulator with jurisdiction over the case.
