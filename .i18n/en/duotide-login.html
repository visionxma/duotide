Duotide Login

You access your Duotide account through the trading platform, using your registered email and the password you set when you signed up. If you forgot your password, use the recovery option on the login screen itself — the reset link is sent to the email you registered with.

Don't have an account yet?

Signing up is free and takes just a few minutes. Once you have created the account, the same email and password let you log in to the platform.

Can't log in?

  • Check your email address. Login uses the address you registered with, not a username.
  • Check your keyboard. Caps Lock being on is the most common reason a password is rejected.
  • Complete verification. Accounts with pending documents may have limited access.
  • Try another browser. Ad-blocking extensions sometimes stop the login screen from loading.

Never give out your password by email, chat or message. Logging in only happens on the official platform, and no legitimate support agent will ask for your password.
