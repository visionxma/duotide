Duotide withdrawals

Withdrawing is the only test that truly matters. A slick platform, low spreads and friendly support are worth nothing if the money does not come back. This page shows how to request a withdrawal, what usually holds it up and how to test it before risking a large amount.

How to request a withdrawal

  1. Go to the funds area of your account, with your profile already verified (KYC completed);
  2. Choose the withdrawal method — usually the same one used for the deposit;
  3. Enter the amount, respecting the required minimum;
  4. Confirm with your two-factor authentication code;
  5. Keep the ticket number and follow the status until it is confirmed.

What holds up a withdrawal

  • Incomplete KYC: a pending or rejected document blocks withdrawals, even with an available balance;
  • Active bonus: trading volume requirements are the most frequent cause of refused withdrawals;
  • Name mismatch: the destination account must be in the same name (in Brazil, the same CPF taxpayer number) as the registered account;
  • Amount below the minimum: common and easy to fix;
  • Different method from the deposit: many brokers require funds to go back the same way they came in;
  • Security review: the first withdrawal usually goes through a manual review.

The first-withdrawal test. Before depositing any amount that matters to you: deposit the minimum, trade a little, and request a withdrawal the following week. Time it. If the money comes back within the promised time frame, you have the most valuable information there is about this broker — and you paid little for it. If it does not, you lost the minimum instead of your savings.

If the withdrawal does not arrive

  1. Open a formal ticket through the official channel and keep the ticket number;
  2. Record everything: screenshots of your balance, the request, the conversations and the dates;
  3. Check whether there is an active bonus or pending KYC on your account;
  4. If the problem persists, file a complaint on consumidor.gov.br and on Reclame Aqui (Brazilian consumer complaint platforms), or with the consumer protection bodies in your country;
  5. For significant amounts, seek legal advice — and be aware that, without CVM authorization, there is no Brazilian regulator to turn to.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a Duotide withdrawal take?

The time frame varies with the method and the security check. See the platform's transactions area for the current processing time. As an industry benchmark, withdrawals usually take anywhere from a few hours to a few business days.

Why was my withdrawal not approved?

The most common causes are incomplete identity verification (KYC), an active bonus with an unmet trading volume requirement, bank details that do not match the account holder, or an amount below the minimum.

Can I withdraw to someone else's account?

No. Brokers require the destination account to belong to the same person who registered, under anti-money-laundering rules. Trying to do so usually gets the account frozen.
