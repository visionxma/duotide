Duotide minimum deposit

The right question is not "what is the minimum", but "how much should I put in at the start". The answer to the second is the same at any broker that is not regulated where you live: the smallest amount possible.

Where to check the current amount

The minimum varies by payment method and changes without notice. The reliable source is the transactions area inside the platform, once you have signed up. Any figure published by a third-party site — including this one — may be out of date by the time you read it.

Commonly accepted methods

  • Pix (Brazil's instant payment system) and bank transfer, for services that cater to Brazil;
  • Credit or debit card;
  • E-wallets;
  • Cryptocurrencies, common at brokers with an offshore structure.

Keep track of the method you used: most brokers require withdrawals to go back the same way the money came in, and this often catches people by surprise.

Treat your first deposit as a testing cost, not an investment. Its purpose is to answer a single question: "does this broker return my money on time?". Deposit the minimum, trade a little, withdraw and time it. Only then decide whether it is worth depositing a meaningful amount.

Beware of first-deposit bonuses

The offer looks good, and it is exactly where many people get stuck: the bonus almost always requires a minimum trading volume before any withdrawal is released. Accepting it means you cannot withdraw your own money until you hit that target — and on a test deposit, that defeats the purpose of the test.
