How to open a Duotide account

Signing up takes just a few minutes; it is verification that determines when you can actually start trading. Below is the full process and the details that most often get documents rejected.

Step by step

  1. Sign-up: enter your email and create a unique password that you do not use for any other service;
  2. Email confirmation: click the link you receive to validate the address;
  3. Personal details: fill them in exactly as they appear on your ID document — mismatches are the No. 1 cause of rejection;
  4. Document upload (KYC): photo ID and proof of address;
  5. Wait for the review: anywhere from a few hours to a few business days;
  6. Turn on 2FA: do this before depositing any money;
  7. Test deposit: start with the minimum and test a withdrawal before depositing more.

Documents: what usually goes wrong

  • Cropped or blurry photo — upload the whole document, with the edges visible;
  • Flash glare over the details — take the photo in soft light, without shiny lamination reflecting;
  • Old proof of address — it usually has to be issued within the last 3 months;
  • Proof of address in someone else's name — it must be in your name;
  • Name mismatch between the sign-up form and the document, including abbreviations;
  • Expired document — an expired driver's license is usually rejected.

Before your first deposit, do two things: turn on app-based two-factor authentication (not SMS) and read the withdrawal clause in the terms of use. That is five minutes that prevent the two most common problems — a hacked account and locked-up money.

Always refuse

A fee to activate your account, a request for your password by phone or chat, and an offer from someone to "trade for you" are all three signs of fraud. No legitimate broker does this — and we never ask for anything like that either.

Frequently asked questions

What documents does Duotide require?

The industry standard is a photo ID (in Brazil, an RG identity card or CNH driver's license; elsewhere, a passport, national ID card or driver's license), a recent proof of address and, in some cases, a selfie holding the document.

How long does verification take?

The review usually takes anywhere from a few hours to a few business days. A legible, valid document with details identical to those on your sign-up speeds up the process.

Do I have to pay to open an account?

No. Opening an account is free across the industry. A charge to "unlock" or "activate" your registration is a sign of fraud and should be refused.
