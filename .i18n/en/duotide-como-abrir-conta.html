How to open a Duotide account

Opening your Duotide account is simple and takes just a few minutes. It's only 3 steps:

  1. Enter your email: click “Create account”, enter your email and create a password.
  2. Confirm your details: fill in your name and personal details and upload a photo of your ID.
  3. Done, start trading: make your first deposit and start trading in your browser or in the app.
