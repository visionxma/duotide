Risk warning

You can lose your entire invested capital. Do not invest money you need to live on, nor funds set aside as an emergency reserve, rent, debt payments or loan installments.

Market risk

Leveraged and derivative products amplify gains and losses. A small change in the asset's price can wipe out the position. This risk belongs to the product: it exists even if the platform works perfectly, and no security setting eliminates it.

Counterparty risk

Duotide is not authorized by the CVM and declares registration in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG). This means there is no applicable guarantee fund and no Brazilian regulator to turn to in the event of a failure, an account block or an unpaid withdrawal. The risk of the counterparty not honoring its obligations is entirely yours.

Past-performance risk

Past results do not indicate future results. Simulations, backtests and operational demonstrations are scenarios, not predictions. Individual results differ substantially from those shown in promotional materials.

No advisory service

Nothing on this site constitutes a recommendation, offer or investment advice. We are not advisors authorized by the CVM. For personalized guidance, seek a certified, registered professional.

How to reduce your exposure

  • Start with the smallest amount possible and test a withdrawal before depositing more;
  • Turn on two-factor authentication from your very first access;
  • Do not keep more balance with the broker than what you are actually using;
  • Read the bonus terms: they tend to lock withdrawals;
  • Be wary of any promise of guaranteed returns or of a manager who offers to trade on your behalf.
