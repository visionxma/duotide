About this site

This is not Duotide's official website. We are an independent informational portal. We have no corporate ties to the broker, we do not manage client accounts and we do not hold anyone's money.

What we do

We gather and organize public information about Duotide: what the platform offers, how account access works, its regulatory status and what risks an investor takes on. The goal is for you to reach a decision with more information than the broker's own sales page provides.

How we fund ourselves

This site is monetized through affiliation. The sign-up and login buttons point to the partner platform Safirion, and we may earn a commission for sign-ups that originate here. This adds no extra cost for you.

We say this above the fold on every page, not in fine print in the footer, because it changes how you should read the rest of the content. A site that earns money per sign-up has an incentive to downplay risk — knowing that is part of your own assessment.

What we do not do

  • We do not give investment advice. We are not advisors authorized by the CVM, and nothing here is a buy or sell recommendation;
  • We do not promise returns. Any site that promises guaranteed gains in financial markets is lying;
  • We do not publish a made-up score or ranking. Without documented testing of our own, we do not assign a rating;
  • We do not trade on your behalf and we never ask for access to your account — if anyone does this in our name, it is a scam.

Corrections

Broker information changes fast: fees, assets, time frames and even jurisdiction. If you find something outdated or incorrect on this page, write to contato@duotide.com.br — we will fix it and record the date of the change.
