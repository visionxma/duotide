Duotide Broker

Duotide markets itself to traders who follow short-term market moves. This page brings together public information about the platform, account access and the risks involved.

Explore the platform

How it works

Duotide operates as an online broker: the whole relationship takes place online, from sign-up to withdrawal. The process is as follows:

  1. Sign-up: personal details and password creation;
  2. Verification (KYC): uploading an ID document and proof of address;
  3. Deposit: initial funding through the methods the broker accepts;
  4. Trading: in the browser or through the app;
  5. Withdrawal: requested from the dashboard, subject to security checks.

Available assets

As publicly disclosed by the broker:

  • Cryptocurrencies
  • Currency pairs (forex)
  • Global indices
  • Commodities

Duotide's messaging is built around reading order flow and timing entries and exits, which suggests an audience of active traders rather than long-term investors.

Deposits and withdrawals

Accepted methods, minimum amounts and processing times vary and change frequently — which is why we do not reproduce figures here: always check the transactions area of the platform itself, which is the only up-to-date source.

The practical point worth stressing: make a small withdrawal early. A withdrawal paid on time is the most objective indicator of the operational health of a broker that is not regulated where you live.

Regulatory status

Duotide states that it is registered in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG) and is not authorized by the CVM, Brazil's securities regulator. Jurisdictions like this have far lower capital and supervision requirements than Brazil or Europe.

Before you continue: this page is for information only and is not Duotide's official website.

Duotide is not authorized by the CVM, Brazil's securities regulator, to offer brokerage services to residents of Brazil, and it operates under registration in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG). This means you do not benefit from the investor protection mechanisms that apply to institutions authorized in Brazil.
