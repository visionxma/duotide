What is Duotide?

Duotide is an online broker that positions itself around short-term trading focused on market flow. Access is 100% online, both in your browser and on your phone, and account opening follows the industry-standard process: sign-up, identity verification (KYC, or Know Your Customer) and deposit.

Duotide's messaging is built around reading order flow and timing entries and exits, which suggests an audience of active traders rather than long-term investors.

Before you continue: this page is for information only and is not Duotide's official website.

Duotide is not authorized by the CVM, Brazil's securities regulator, to offer brokerage services to residents of Brazil, and it operates under registration in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG). This means you do not benefit from the investor protection mechanisms that apply to institutions authorized in Brazil.

Points to watch before investing

  • If you want regulatory protection in Brazil, check whether the broker is authorized by the CVM — this one is not. Wherever you live, also check the register of your own country's financial regulator.
  • Read the terms of use, paying close attention to the rules on withdrawals, inactivity fees and bonuses.
  • Be wary of any promise of guaranteed returns: there is no such thing in financial markets.
  • Turn on 2FA (two-factor authentication) and never share verification codes with anyone.
  • Start with a small amount and only increase it after testing deposits, withdrawals and support.

How to open a Duotide account

The process is the same one used by most offshore brokers and takes just a few minutes:

  1. Go to the broker's sign-up page;
  2. Enter your email and personal details and create a strong password;
  3. Confirm the email you receive;
  4. Upload your documents for identity verification (KYC);
  5. Turn on two-factor authentication before depositing;
  6. Make a small first deposit to test withdrawals and customer support.

Step 6 is the most important and the most overlooked: test a small withdrawal before depositing a significant amount. It is the most objective test of the operational health of any broker that is not regulated where you live.
