Duotide App

How to use Duotide on your phone, what the app lets you do and — above all — how to avoid falling for a fake version.

A rule with no exceptions: install only from Google Play or the App Store, searching for the broker's name inside the store itself. Never install an APK received by link, email, WhatsApp or Telegram — not even if it comes from someone claiming to be from support. It is the most common scam in the industry.

How to get it

  1. Open your device's official store (Google Play on Android, the App Store on iOS);
  2. Search for the broker's name inside the store, not in a search engine;
  3. Check the developer and the number of reviews before installing;
  4. If there is no official app, use your phone's browser at the broker's address;
  5. Log in and turn on 2FA before making any deposit.

What you can do on your phone

  • Follow quotes and charts in real time;
  • Open and close positions;
  • Check your balance, open positions and history;
  • Request deposits and withdrawals;
  • Set up price alerts.

Precautions when using your phone

  • Avoid trading on public Wi-Fi networks;
  • Keep your operating system and the app up to date;
  • Use a screen lock with biometrics;
  • Turn off notification previews so 2FA codes are not exposed on the lock screen;
  • Periodically revoke old sessions in your account dashboard.
