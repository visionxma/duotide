Is Duotide safe?

The question hides two different issues, and confusing them is the most expensive mistake an investor makes: technical security (your account being hacked) and regulatory protection (having someone to turn to if the broker does not pay). They are separate things.

Technical security

Encryption in transit, two-factor authentication and identity verification are standard in the industry and reduce the risk of someone accessing your account without permission. They are real measures and you should use them — especially 2FA, which is up to you.

But notice what they cover: unauthorized access. They have nothing to do with whether the broker honors a withdrawal.

Regulatory protection

This is the point that determines the real risk. Duotide states that it is registered in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG) and is not authorized by the CVM, Brazil's securities regulator. There is no compensation fund, no segregation of client assets that you can verify, and no Brazilian authority to turn to if there is a problem.

In other words: however robust the encryption, the counterparty risk remains entirely with you.

Before you continue: this page is for information only and is not Duotide's official website.

Duotide is not authorized by the CVM to offer brokerage services to residents of Brazil, and it operates under registration in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG). This means you do not benefit from the investor protection mechanisms that apply to institutions authorized in Brazil.

How to reduce your exposure

  • Turn on app-based 2FA as soon as you create the account;
  • Keep only the capital you are using at the broker — do not use the account as a custody wallet;
  • Withdraw regularly instead of letting your balance build up on the platform;
  • Avoid deposit bonuses unless you have read the conditions: they often lock withdrawals;
  • Keep records of everything: receipts, ticket numbers and screenshots of every transaction;
  • Never trade with money you need — rent, debt payments or your emergency fund.

The risk no measure can eliminate

Leveraged products can wipe out your entire capital in a short time, even with the platform working perfectly. This risk belongs to the product, not the broker, and no security setting can remove it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my money protected at Duotide?

There is no Brazilian institutional guarantee. The broker is not authorized by the CVM and no compensation fund applies. The counterparty risk is entirely yours.

What is 2FA and why should I turn it on?

It is two-factor authentication: in addition to your password, it requires a temporary code. It blocks access to your account even if someone finds out your password. Always turn it on.
